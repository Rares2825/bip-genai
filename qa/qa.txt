📋 INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE QA (Quality Assurance) & EVALUATION TEAM
====================================================================

YOUR ROLE: 
You are the "Devil's Advocate". In a legal/regulatory system (like DEW21 AGBs), the biggest risk is AI "hallucination" (making up rules that do not exist). Your job is to prevent this!

YOUR TASKS FOR DAYS 1-2 (While developers write the core code):
1. Read the provided DEW21 documents (Terms & Conditions / Federal Network Agency).
2. Create an Excel file (you can save it in this folder, e.g., `dew21_tests.xlsx`).
3. Fill the spreadsheet with at least 30 realistic questions that customers or employees might ask.
   Your table columns should be:
   | ID | Question (Ger/Eng) | Expected Correct Answer | Source Document & Page | Test Result (Pass/Fail) |

YOUR TASKS FOR DAYS 3-4 (After receiving the UI prototype):
1. Run the Streamlit application on your local machine.
2. Take each question from your Excel file and ask the AI.
3. Compare the AI's response with your "Expected Correct Answer".
4. CRUCIAL: Verify if the AI cited the correct source (document and page)!
5. Make a list of all the questions the AI failed or hallucinated on, and give it to the Backend team so they can fix the system prompt.

Golden Rule: It is much better for the AI to say "I don't know" than to invent a fake law!
