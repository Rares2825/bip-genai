Question	Expected Answer	Category
How can I conclude a contract with DEW21?	The contract is concluded when DEW21 confirms it in writing, specifying the expected delivery start date. The actual start depends on the completion of all required steps such as termination of the previous contract and supplier approval.	Contract
When does the energy supply start?	The supply begins on the confirmed delivery date after all technical and contractual conditions are fulfilled. No supply occurs before the expiry of the legal withdrawal period unless explicitly requested by the customer.	Contract
Can I withdraw from the contract after signing?	Customers have a statutory right of withdrawal according to §§ 355 and 356 BGB. Supply will not begin before this period expires unless the customer explicitly requests early delivery.	Contract
How are electricity prices determined?	The total price consists of a base price and variable components such as grid charges, taxes, levies, and other regulatory costs that are applied according to consumption.	Pricing
Can prices change during the contract?	DEW21 may adjust prices based on cost changes. Price changes must be communicated in writing at least six weeks before they take effect.	Pricing
How will I be informed about price changes?	Customers are notified in writing at least six weeks before a price change and the information is also published on the DEW21 website.	Pricing
What payment methods are available?	Customers can pay via SEPA direct debit or bank transfer as specified in the contract.	Payment
What happens if I do not pay my bill on time?	If the customer defaults, DEW21 may charge reminder fees, interest, and collection costs including legal expenses under § 288 BGB.	Payment
What is the minimum debt required for disconnection?	Supply may be interrupted if the outstanding amount is at least €100 or equals twice the monthly installment, including reminder and collection costs.	Payment
How long before service is interrupted?	The customer must receive at least four weeks’ advance notice before disconnection, and the network operator is informed eight working days in advance.	Payment
Are disputed amounts counted toward disconnection?	No, disputed or not yet due claims are excluded from the calculation of the minimum debt required for interruption.	Payment
Can the energy supply be interrupted for non-payment?	Yes, DEW21 may suspend supply after prior warning if the required debt threshold is reached.	Payment
Are there additional charges for late payment?	Yes, additional costs may include reminder fees, interest, and legal or collection costs incurred by DEW21.	Payment
How can I restore the service after interruption?	The customer must pay all outstanding amounts and the costs of interruption and reconnection, which are borne by the customer.	Payment
How often will I receive a bill?	Electricity consumption is generally billed every 12 months unless interim billing is agreed.	Billing
How is my energy consumption calculated?	Consumption is measured through meter readings and calculated based on actual usage during the billing period.	Billing
What happens if the meter reading is incorrect?	If consumption appears incorrect, the customer can request verification of the metering device and may defer payment under certain conditions.	Billing
How can I dispute a bill?	Customers can object to invoices if there is a significant deviation or error and request a review or meter verification.	Billing
What voltage does DEW21 supply?	Electricity is supplied as three-phase current at approximately 400V or 230V, or alternating current at approximately 230V, with a frequency of about 50 Hz.	Technical
How is gas consumption converted to kWh?	Gas consumption in cubic meters is converted into kWh using a conversion factor based on calorific value and condition number defined by the network operator.	Technical
Does DEW21 perform a credit check?	DEW21 may request creditworthiness information from SCHUFA or Creditreform and receive score values for risk assessment.	Credit Check
What data is shared with SCHUFA?	Personal data such as name, address, payment behavior, and contract information may be processed and shared for credit assessment.	Credit Check
What scoring method does SCHUFA use?	SCHUFA uses a statistical method called logistic regression to calculate score values based on historical data and patterns.	Credit Check
Can a SCHUFA score alone reject my contract?	No, a SCHUFA score alone is not sufficient to reject a contract; the final decision is made by the contractual partner using additional information.	Credit Check
What data does SCHUFA NOT use in scoring?	SCHUFA does not use sensitive data such as nationality, ethnic origin, or political and religious beliefs in score calculation.	Credit Check
How long does SCHUFA store data?	Personal data is generally stored for three years from completion, with some exceptions depending on the type of data.	Data Protection
How long does Creditreform store data?	Data is generally stored for four years, and after completion of a case it is typically deleted three years later unless further storage is necessary.	Data Protection
What rights do I have regarding my data?	Customers have rights under GDPR including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and objection.	Data Protection
Can I object to data processing?	Yes, customers may object to data processing under Article 21 GDPR if there are specific personal reasons.	Data Protection
 
Where can I file a complaint about data handling?	Customers can contact the responsible data protection authority or the organization’s data protection officer.
